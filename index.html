Our Story

An introduction, then an education

Ours began the way many good stories in our families do — with an introduction, not a meet-cute. Two families, a conversation, and a quiet hope that it might work.

What neither of us expected was how quickly "getting to know each other" turned into something we looked forward to every day — long phone calls that ran past midnight, terrible jokes that became our jokes, and the slow, certain realisation that this wasn't an arrangement anymore. It was simply us.

We went from strangers, to a couple, to two people who can't quite remember what conversation felt like before the other was on the other end of it. Arranged by our families, chosen by us.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What should I wear?

Mehndi & Nutair is festive-casual — bring the outfit you don't mind henna touching. Haldi calls for something bright (yellow is always welcome) that you're happy to see stained. The wedding is traditional formal — think your best festive attire.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, Le Oval Hotel & Resort has on-site parking for all three events. Valet will be available on the wedding day.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely — we'd love to have the little ones celebrate with us at every event.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Please check your invitation or reach out to us directly — we're keeping the guest list close, so we want to confirm with each family individually.

Is accommodation available at the venue?

Le Oval Hotel & Resort offers on-site rooms. Let us know if you'd like a recommendation or help booking, and we'll point you in the right direction.
